CyberSniff Physical-Fault Model Experiment
==========================================

Baseline: Full-feature model comparison.
Experiment: Eight selected features.

Mean results:
Random Forest: Accuracy 69.4%, Fault Recall 72.1%.
Extra Trees: Accuracy 62.5%, Fault Recall 58.3%.
HistGradientBoosting: Accuracy 73.7%, Fault Recall 80.8%.

KA05 results:
Random Forest: Accuracy 48.75%.
Extra Trees: Accuracy 0%.
HistGradientBoosting: Accuracy 75% (60 of 80 samples detected).

Current best candidate:
HistGradientBoosting using eight selected features.

Limitations:
Performance varies by bearing. Further independent validation is required.
These results do not establish production readiness.
