CyberSniff Physical-Fault Model Experiment
==========================================

Latest experiment: grouped cross-validation comparing
HistGradientBoosting with four and eight selected features.

Latest grouped-CV results:
- HGB, 4 features: mean fold accuracy 82.92%;
  mean fold fault recall 84.58%.
- HGB, 8 features: mean fold accuracy 75.21%;
  mean fold fault recall 82.08%.

Combined out-of-fold results:
- HGB, 4 features: 203/240 faults detected; 37 missed.
- HGB, 8 features: 197/240 faults detected; 43 missed.

Historical comparison (older experiment; not directly comparable
without confirming identical evaluation procedures):
- Random Forest: accuracy 69.4%, fault recall 72.1%.
- Extra Trees: accuracy 62.5%, fault recall 58.3%.
- Eight-feature HistGradientBoosting: accuracy 73.7%,
  fault recall 80.8%.
- Historical KA05 results: Random Forest accuracy 48.75%,
  Extra Trees accuracy 0%, HistGradientBoosting detected
  60/80 fault samples (75% recall).

Current leading experimental candidate:
HistGradientBoosting using four selected features, based on the
latest grouped-CV metrics. This is not proof of independent
generalization or production readiness.

Limitations:
K001-K003 contain only Healthy samples, while KA03-KA05 contain
only Physical_Fault samples. Bearing identity is confounded with
class. Further validation on independently labeled bearings is
required.
