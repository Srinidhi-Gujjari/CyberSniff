CyberSniff Model Work Status
============================

1. CyberSniff model: trained and evaluated.
2. Physical-fault models: trained and compared.
3. Eight-feature experiment: completed.
4. Best current candidate: HistGradientBoosting with eight features.
5. Mean accuracy: 73.7%.
6. Mean fault recall: 80.8%.
7. KA05 fault recall: 75% (60 of 80 samples detected).

Remaining work:
- Validate on additional independent bearings.
- Investigate bearing-specific differences.
- Do not claim production readiness without further validation.

Validation findings
===================
The dataset contains six bearing IDs with 80 samples each.
Performance varies by held-out bearing.
K002 and KA05 remain challenging for some or all candidate models.
The current dataset does not provide additional independent bearings
for external validation.
Do not claim production readiness based on these results alone.

Bearing-label limitation
========================
All samples from K001, K002, and K003 are Healthy.
All samples from KA03, KA04, and KA05 are Physical_Fault.
Bearing identity is therefore confounded with the class label.
Leave-one-bearing-out results alone cannot establish robust
generalization to independent unseen bearings.
Independent labeled bearing data is needed for stronger validation.

Eight-feature HistGradientBoosting grouped validation
====================================================
Mean fold accuracy: 75.21%.
Mean fold fault recall: 82.08%.
Combined out-of-fold fault recall: 82.08%.
Combined out-of-fold healthy recall: 68.33%.
Fold accuracy ranged from 41.88% to 100%.
The model remains experimental. Independent bearing validation
is required before production use.
