CyberSniff Model Work Status
============================

Current leading experimental candidate:
HistGradientBoosting with four selected features.

Latest grouped-CV results:
- HGB 4-feature: mean fold accuracy 82.92%.
- HGB 4-feature: mean fold fault recall 84.58%.
- Combined out-of-fold accuracy: 82.92%.
- Faults detected: 203 of 240.
- Faults missed: 37 of 240.
- Healthy samples correctly classified: 195 of 240.
- False alarms on healthy samples: 45 of 240.

Eight-feature HGB comparison:
- Mean fold accuracy: 75.21%.
- Mean fold fault recall: 82.08%.
- Faults detected: 197 of 240.
- Faults missed: 43 of 240.

Bearing-label limitation
========================
K001, K002, and K003 contain only Healthy samples.
KA03, KA04, and KA05 contain only Physical_Fault samples.
Bearing identity is therefore confounded with class.
These grouped-CV results do not establish generalization to
independent bearings. Additional independently labeled bearing
data is needed. The model remains experimental, not production-ready.
