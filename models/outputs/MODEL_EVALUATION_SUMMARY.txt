CyberSniff Model Evaluation Summary
==================================

- Further validation is needed before claiming real-world reliability.

Historical comparison figures
=============================
An older experiment reported Random Forest at 69.4% accuracy and
72.1% fault recall, Extra Trees at 62.5% and 58.3%, and
eight-feature HistGradientBoosting at 73.7% and 80.8%.
These historical figures are retained for context and are not
directly comparable to the latest grouped-CV results without
confirming that evaluation procedures match.

Limitations
===========
All Healthy samples come from K001-K003; all Physical_Fault
samples come from KA03-KA05. Bearing identity is confounded with
class. Independent labeled bearing data is required before making
claims of robust generalization or production readiness.
