CyberSniff Model Evaluation Summary
==================================

CyberSniff:
- Model trained successfully.
- Previously reported test accuracy: 100%.
- Further validation is needed before claiming real-world reliability.

Physical-fault detection:
- Random Forest fails completely on unseen bearing KA05 (0% accuracy).
- Extra Trees also fails on KA05 (0% accuracy).
- HistGradientBoosting achieves 100% on KA03 and KA04, but 0% on KA05.
- Performance varies substantially between held-out bearings.

Conclusion:
- Model training and evaluation scripts are implemented.
- Physical-fault detection is not yet reliable across unseen bearings.
- Further investigation and validation are required before deployment.
